Your vanilla works out at grams per cubic inch of finished cake. Chocolate is 11.14 and red velvet 13.09, which is why one shared setting across all three would misprice red velvet by about 16 per cent.

Any other cake is grams per cubic inch × area × total height, where area is πD²/4 round and D² square. Nothing is read off a table, so there is no largest size.
