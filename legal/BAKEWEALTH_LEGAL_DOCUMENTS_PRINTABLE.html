PART I: PRIVACY POLICY

Plain-English Summary: This policy describes how BakeWealth collects, handles, stores, and protects information for bakeries, their employees, and their customers. We do not sell data. We use industry-standard encryption, strict multi-tenant separation, and trusted enterprise processors.

1. Introduction

BakeWealth (accessible at bakewealthinternational.com) is an all-in-one bakery management, costing, and bookkeeping application operated by Iye Ibe Achem ("we", "us", "our"). BakeWealth enables cake artists, home bakers, and commercial baking enterprises to calculate precise recipe costs, track orders, manage ingredient inventory, scan receipts, and generate accounting statements.

Because you trust us with sensitive information about your craft, proprietary recipes, and business finances, transparency and data protection are fundamental to our service. We process personal and business data in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA 2023), regulations of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), and international standards such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and secure BakeWealth:

  • User & Account Data: Your full name, email address, password (stored solely as a cryptographically salted bcrypt hash), account type (individual or organization), staff roles (owner, manager, baker, customer service), and staff 4-digit PINs.
  • Bakery Profile: Bakery business name, contact phone, contact email, physical address, bakery logo URL, brand color preferences, and custom margin percentages.
  • Customer (Client) Directory: Client names, phone numbers, email addresses, delivery addresses, birthdays (for marketing reminders), and order notes.
  • Recipes & Inventory: Ingredient names, unit costs, stock quantities, reorder thresholds, inventory history logs, recipe formulations, batch weights, and cake application rates.
  • Orders, Quotes & Invoices: Order dates, due dates, tier sizes, shapes, flavors, decorative items, quoted prices, payment logs, and invoice records.
  • Financial Data: Operational expenses, ingredient purchase records, supplier payables, bank transaction logs, and balance sheet opening figures.
  • Payment Transaction Records: Paystack transaction references, idempotency keys, amounts in kobo (NGN), and status logs. We never see or store debit or credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account PINs.
  • AI Scanner Inputs: Uploaded receipt images or bank statement text snippets submitted to our AI features.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser user agents, and security request logs.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect data when you enter it directly into the application, automatically as the system calculates inventory deductions and ledger balances, and via secure server-to-server webhook notifications from Paystack upon payment completion.

5. How We Store and Protect Information

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Bcrypt Password Hashing: Passwords are protected using salt rounds of 12. Plaintext passwords are never accessible.
  • Encryption in Transit: All web and API traffic uses HTTPS with modern TLS 1.2+ encryption.
  • Multi-Tenant Data Isolation: Every database table enforces strict Tenant ID filtering and cascade integrity.
  • Rate Limiting: Sensitive routes (logins, payments, refunds) enforce strict IP rate limiting to stop brute-force attacks.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Hosted on managed PostgreSQL infrastructure on Supabase / AWS (Frankfurt, Germany) with automated backups and security monitoring.

6. Who Can Access Information

  • Bakery Owner: Full access to all modules, financial statements, staff management, and Danger Zone account controls.
  • Production Staff (Bakers): Access to recipes, inventory, order calculator, and production schedules. Cannot view bank transactions, expenses, P&L, or Balance Sheets.
  • Customer Service Staff: Access to client directory, quotes, orders, and invoices. Cannot view recipes, costs, or bank records.
  • Super-Administrator: Platform operator access restricted strictly to aggregate platform statistics (e.g. total tenant count, active users, total revenue) and tenant status moderation. The super-administrator cannot inspect your private customer names, recipes, or financial ledgers.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with verified infrastructure partners:

  • Paystack Payments Limited (Nigeria): Payment gateway for subscriptions and credit packs. Certified PCI-DSS Level 1.
  • Brevo / Sendinblue SAS (France / EU): Delivery of transactional emails, activation tokens, and receipts.
  • Anthropic PBC (USA): Cloud AI processing for receipt and statement parsing. Commercial API terms confirm data is not used for model training.
  • Supabase / AWS (Germany / EU): Cloud database hosting and backups.
  • Cloudflare Inc. (Global): Static asset delivery and DDoS mitigation.

8. Cookies and Storage

BakeWealth does not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies. We use browser sessionStorage solely to hold your active authentication session token and localStorage for caching your interface preferences.

9. Artificial Intelligence Processing

AI features (Receipt Scanner and Bank Statement Importer) are strictly optional. We enforce a daily protective ceiling of 100 AI scans per account to prevent unintended loops. Data processed via our Anthropic Claude integration is not retained for training public AI models.

10. Payments and Financial Information

Subscriptions and credit packs are charged in Nigerian Naira (NGN). All card payments are handled within Paystack's secure interface. BakeWealth operates on a prepaid model: we do not automatically charge your card without your active knowledge.

11. Data Retention

Your records remain accessible for as long as your account is active. If a prepaid subscription ends, your account transitions to the Free tier; your historical recipes, inventory, and orders are preserved. If you delete your account via Settings, all tenant records are permanently purged in an atomic database transaction.

12. User Rights Under NDPA 2023 and GDPR

You have the legal right to access your data, correct inaccuracies, export a complete JSON backup (via Settings → Backup & Data), request account deletion (via Settings → Danger Zone), or lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng. Contact us at info@bakewealthinternational.com for privacy requests.

13. International Data Transfers

Data is transferred to secure cloud facilities in the European Union (Germany and France) and the United States (Anthropic) in compliance with Sections 41–43 of the NDPA 2023 under contractual safeguards and service necessity.

14. Children's Privacy

BakeWealth is commercial business software intended solely for adult business owners and employees aged 18 and older.

15. Policy Changes

We notify users of material privacy updates via dashboard notices or email. Continued use following notice constitutes acceptance.
