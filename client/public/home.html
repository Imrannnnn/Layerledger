Where the money is leaking

Every answer, and what it is costing you.

You love baking. But are you making money from it?

You have just answered ten questions about the money side of your cakes. Some of them you could answer straight away. Others you had to think about, or guess at.

That is the point of them. Every one of those questions is sitting behind a price you have already quoted this year. What a cake costs you in ingredients. What an hour in your kitchen is worth. Whether you are on your own payroll. What you do when a customer says it is too much. Whether you could say, right now, if last month made money.

Your time, your labour, electricity, gas, decoration, packaging and the cost of simply keeping the place open all go into every cake you sell. When those are not counted, you can be busy, taking orders and posting cakes you are proud of, and still not making the profit your business needs.

The problem may not be that you are not selling enough cakes. It may be that you are not costing them properly.

But you can change that.
